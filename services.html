Home / Services

Flexible tuition and alternative education services.

Provision can be tailored around each learner’s needs, starting point, confidence level and referral goals. Support can be delivered online, in person or through a hybrid model depending on suitability and availability.

Core delivery options

  • One-to-one tuition
  • Online, in-person or hybrid
  • Short-term or ongoing support
  • Attendance and progress reporting
S

SEND Tuition

Personalised tuition for learners with special educational needs and disabilities, including ADHD, ASD, sensory processing difficulties, SEMH, communication needs and complex needs where provision is suitable.

  • Adapted pace
  • Clear structure
  • Confidence building
  • Suitability and risk review
A

Alternative Provision

Structured education support for learners who are not currently accessing mainstream education or need interim provision.

  • Out-of-school pupils
  • Awaiting placement
  • Re-engagement support
  • Referrer reporting
1:1

One-to-One Tuition

Individual tuition focused on the learner’s specific gaps, goals, confidence and academic progress.

  • English
  • Maths
  • Science
  • GCSE and Functional Skills
☷

Online Tuition

Safe and flexible online sessions for learners who need access to education from home or another suitable setting.

  • Useful for learners with anxiety or medical barriers
  • Clear expectations for online safety
  • Session notes and attendance records
⌖

In-Person Tuition

Face-to-face tuition in agreed suitable locations, subject to availability, suitability and safeguarding considerations.

  • Home, school or agreed setting
  • Appropriate risk review
  • Professional boundaries
✓

Subject Support

English, Maths, Science, GCSE preparation, Functional Skills and other subjects where suitable tutors are available.

  • Gap identification
  • Revision support
  • Exam technique
How provision is shaped

Support is built around referral goals.

Tuition can focus on subject progress, confidence, consistent routine, re-engagement, exam preparation or maintaining education while longer-term arrangements are being made.
