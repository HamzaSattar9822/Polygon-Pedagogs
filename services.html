Home / Our Services

Flexible tuition and alternative education services.

Provision can be tailored around each learner’s needs, starting point, confidence level and referral goals. Support can be delivered online, in person or through a hybrid model depending on suitability and availability.

Core delivery options

  • One-to-one tuition
  • Online, in-person or hybrid
  • Short-term or ongoing support
  • Attendance and progress reporting
S

SEND Tuition

Personalised tuition for learners with special educational needs and disabilities, including ADHD, ASD, sensory processing difficulties, SEMH, communication needs and complex needs where provision is suitable.

  • Adapted pace
  • Clear structure
  • Confidence building
  • Suitability and risk review
A

Alternative Provision

Structured education support for learners who are not currently accessing mainstream education or need interim provision.

  • Out-of-school pupils
  • Awaiting placement
  • Re-engagement support
  • Referrer reporting
1:1

One-to-One Tuition

Individual tuition focused on the learner’s specific gaps, goals, confidence and academic progress.

  • English
  • Maths
  • Science
  • GCSE and Functional Skills
☷

Online Tuition

Safe and flexible online sessions for learners who need access to education from home or another suitable setting.

  • Useful for learners with anxiety or medical barriers
  • Clear expectations for online safety
  • Session notes and attendance records
⌖

In-Person Tuition

Face-to-face tuition in agreed suitable locations, subject to availability, suitability and safeguarding considerations.

  • Home, school or agreed setting
  • Appropriate risk review
  • Professional boundaries
✓

Subject Support

English, Maths, Science, GCSE preparation, Functional Skills and other subjects where suitable tutors are available.

  • Gap identification
  • Revision support
  • Exam technique
Education partners

Support for schools, councils and education teams

We work with schools, local authorities, virtual schools, SEND teams and alternative provision services to provide flexible education for learners who need additional or interim support.

⌂

Schools

Flexible tuition for learners who need additional or alternative education alongside or outside mainstream school.

◈

Local authorities and virtual schools

Structured provision for children who require interim or personalised education arranged through local authority teams.

✦

SEND and inclusion teams

SEND-informed tuition and mentoring for learners with additional needs, including those with EHCPs where suitable.

↗

Alternative provision and outreach teams

Collaborative support for AP, hospital education and outreach teams seeking safe, personalised learning.

For professional partners

What professional partners receive

✓Tutor matching based on learner needs
✓Individualised tuition and mentoring
✓Flexible home, library, community or online delivery
✓Attendance and engagement tracking
✓Session notes and progress reports
✓Communication with named education professionals
✓Safeguarding-led delivery
How provision is shaped

Support is built around referral goals.

Tuition can focus on subject progress, confidence, consistent routine, re-engagement, exam preparation or maintaining education while longer-term arrangements are being made.

✓Initial learner profile and goal review
✓Personalised teaching approach
✓Attendance and engagement tracking
✓Progress updates and recommendations

Discuss the support you need

Whether you are a professional referrer or a parent or carer, we can help you explore the right next step for the learner.
