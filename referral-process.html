Home / Referral Process

A clear and professional referral journey.

We aim to make referrals simple, secure and efficient. The process helps us understand the learner’s needs, review suitability and arrange safe, appropriate support.

Start here

Professional referrers can complete the referral form. Parents and carers can use the parent enquiry form for an initial discussion.

Submit referral or enquiry

Complete the professional referral form or parent enquiry form with learner details, education status, subjects, support needs and key safeguarding or suitability information.

Initial review

We review learner needs, current education status, referral goals, requested hours, delivery method, documents and risk information.

Plan support

We agree the delivery method, subject focus, frequency, safeguarding expectations, communication route and reporting arrangements.

Tutor matching

A suitable tutor is identified based on subject knowledge, experience, availability, learner profile and delivery requirements.

Tuition begins

Sessions begin with clear expectations around attendance, communication, professional boundaries, session notes and learning goals.

Progress review

Attendance updates, progress summaries and next-step recommendations are shared with the agreed contact.

What happens after a form is submitted?

Enquiry received

The submitted information is securely received by the Polygon Pedagogs team.

Initial review

We review the learner’s needs, requested support, location, availability and any information relevant to suitability or safeguarding.

Follow-up contact

A member of the team contacts the parent, carer or professional referrer to clarify requirements and discuss possible support arrangements.

Suitability and next steps

Where the service appears suitable, we confirm the proposed tuition format, subject support, schedule, reporting expectations and next steps for tutor matching.

Please note: Submitting a form does not guarantee a placement. Support begins only after suitability, availability, safeguarding requirements and service arrangements have been confirmed.
