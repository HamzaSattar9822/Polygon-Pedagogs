Home / Safeguarding

Safeguarding and learner welfare are central to our work.

Many learners referred for tuition or alternative education support may have experienced disruption, anxiety, exclusion, medical needs, SEND-related barriers, complex needs or reduced confidence in education. Safeguarding must be visible, practical and built into daily practice.

Important

If a child is in immediate danger, call 999. For urgent safeguarding concerns, contact the Designated Safeguarding Lead using the details that should be added before launch.

Safeguarding statement

Safe provision starts before tuition begins.

Polygon Pedagogs is committed to promoting the safety and welfare of children and young people accessing its services. Tutors are expected to follow safeguarding procedures, maintain professional boundaries and report concerns appropriately.

Before launch, this page should be checked against the business safeguarding policy and reviewed by a competent safeguarding professional or DSL-level person.

Safeguarding in practice

How safeguarding is reflected across provision.

1

Safer recruitment

Tutors are appointed through suitability checks, identity checks, right-to-work checks, references, DBS checks where appropriate, and safeguarding onboarding.

2

Professional conduct

Tutors are expected to follow a code of conduct covering boundaries, communication, attendance, reporting, online safety and confidentiality.

3

Concern reporting

Any safeguarding or welfare concern should be recorded and escalated through the agreed safeguarding pathway without delay.

4

Online safety

Online sessions should use appropriate platforms, professional communication and clear expectations for learners, tutors and families.

5

Attendance monitoring

Missed sessions, disengagement and welfare worries should be communicated through the agreed route.

6

Data protection

Learner information should be collected only where needed, stored securely and shared only with appropriate people for legitimate safeguarding or education purposes.
