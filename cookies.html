Essential cookies

When staff sign in to the admin inbox, an HTTP-only session cookie is set so the browser can stay signed in securely. This cookie is required for the admin area to work.

Local storage

We store a simple consent flag in local storage after you accept the cookie banner, so the banner does not keep appearing on every visit.

Analytics and marketing

This draft site does not currently load third-party analytics or advertising cookies. If those are added later, this notice should be updated and additional consent obtained where required.

Managing cookies

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing the admin session cookie will sign you out of the admin inbox.
