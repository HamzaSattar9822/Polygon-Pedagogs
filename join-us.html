Join Polygon Pedagogues as a tutor

We are looking for reliable, professional and learner-focused tutors who can provide high-quality tuition and mentoring to children and young people across a range of educational settings.

Tutor standards

  • Subject confidence
  • Professional communication
  • Safeguarding awareness
  • Reliable session reporting
Recruitment

Who we are looking for

We welcome applications from tutors who can support learners with care, professionalism and clear educational purpose.

Q

Qualified teachers and experienced tutors

Professionals with a strong grounding in teaching, tutoring or mentoring children and young people.

S

English, Maths and Science specialists

Subject specialists who can build confidence and progress in core curriculum areas.

✦

SEND and SEMH-experienced practitioners

Tutors who understand adaptive teaching, emotional needs and inclusive classroom practice.

G

Functional Skills and GCSE tutors

Tutors able to support exam preparation, resits and practical next-step learning pathways.

◎

Online or in-person delivery

Tutors able to deliver sessions online, in person, or across both formats where suitable.

✓

Safeguarding and inclusive practice

Professionals committed to safeguarding, professional boundaries and inclusive support.

What the role may involve

  • One-to-one tuition and mentoring.
  • Supporting learners outside mainstream education.
  • Delivering sessions in homes, libraries, community venues or online.
  • Adapting teaching to individual learning needs.
  • Recording attendance and session progress.
  • Communicating professionally with the Polygon Pedagogues team.

Essential requirements

✓Relevant teaching, tutoring or subject experience
✓Appropriate qualifications
✓Right to work in the UK
✓Enhanced DBS check or willingness to complete one
✓Suitable references and identity checks
✓Strong safeguarding awareness
✓Reliable communication and professional conduct

Applications are reviewed carefully. Submission does not guarantee work, placement or specific weekly hours.
