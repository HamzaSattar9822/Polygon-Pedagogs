Professional referral support for schools, councils and education teams.

Polygon Pedagogs works with schools, local authorities, SEND teams, inclusion teams, virtual schools, alternative provision teams and education professionals who need safe, flexible and structured tuition for learners.

Professional referrers

  • Schools and academies
  • Local authorities
  • SEND and inclusion teams
  • Virtual schools and AP teams

What we can provide

  • Referral review and suitability discussion.
  • Tutor matching based on subject, learner needs and delivery method.
  • Online, in-person or hybrid tuition where suitable.
  • Attendance updates and session notes.
  • Progress summaries and next-step recommendations.
  • Communication with named contacts where agreed.

Information that helps at referral stage

  • Current education status and attendance pattern.
  • Subject needs, year group and requested hours.
  • Relevant SEND, EHCP, IEP or pupil passport information.
  • Safeguarding, medical, behaviour or risk information relevant to safe delivery.
  • Preferred start date, delivery method and reporting expectations.
Professional standards

Clear communication from referral to review.

The aim is to make the process straightforward for referrers while keeping safeguarding, learner welfare and data handling central.

Referral received

The professional referral form collects key information securely.

Suitability review

Needs, risks, delivery method and tutor availability are considered.

Provision plan

Subjects, hours, goals, reporting and communication routes are agreed.

Tuition starts

Sessions begin with attendance, engagement and welfare expectations in place.

Review

Progress updates help referrers monitor provision and plan next steps.
